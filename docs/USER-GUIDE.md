# MyMilo user guide

What Milo can do, how to ask, and where to look when something
puzzles you. This guide is also a page on your MyMilo site
(`/guide`) and a section inside the Android app (**Help &
guide**) — all three carry the same content.

## Quick start

1. **Connect your phone.** On the [Devices page](OPERATIONS.md#devices),
   create a token, then paste it into the app's Connect screen.
   (Sign-in pairing — no pasting — is on the roadmap.)
2. **Ask anything.** Type in the message box, or tap the
   microphone and talk. Short or long — Milo keeps the thread.
3. **Summon Milo anywhere.** Once Milo is your assistant
   (app drawer → Assistant), long-press Home or swipe up from
   a bottom corner and just speak.
4. **Run a skill on purpose.** Open the Skills catalogue, pick
   one, and tell it what to work on.
5. **Check the sources.** Under each answer, Milo names what
   it used — a skill, your memory, the web, live market data.
   Nothing used, nothing claimed.

## Skills

Skills are Milo's playbooks — deep know-how it applies when
you ask. Some start on their own when your words match their
triggers; **all of them can be run on purpose** from the app's
Skills screen or by naming one. The full catalogue, with a
plain-language description and an example for every skill:
[SKILLS.md](SKILLS.md).

## Use cases

- **Start the day briefed** — “Brief me on the market today”
- **Understand a stock** — “Analyze AAPL stock for me, step by step”
- **See your money clearly** — “Help me with my household budget”
- **Fly for less** — “Find me the cheapest way to fly Calgary to Mumbai in December”
- **Write it in your voice** — “Write a polite email declining a meeting”
- **Code that comes with proof** — “Write a function that validates Canadian postal codes, and verify it”
- **A memory that works for you** — “Remember that my passport renews in March”
- **Hands-free, anywhere** — long-press Home and ask “What is 15% of 240?”

## Questions, answered

**Where do answers come from?** From your own MyMilo server —
small, fast local models for everyday questions, stronger
routes when a task needs them, plus live web search and
market data when freshness matters. Every answer lists its
sources.

**What works without internet?** Calculations and unit
conversions (instant, on the phone), all your saved chats,
and the skills list. Questions that need the server are kept
and answered when you're back.

**What does Milo remember?** Facts you share and the shape of
past conversations, kept on your server — never sold, never
used for ads (there are no ads). Review and delete remembered
facts from your profile.

**How do updates arrive?** The app checks quietly and tells
you in the drawer under Updates. Download, then Install —
Android asks for your tap on the installer; that's the
platform's rule for apps outside the Play Store.

**Who else can use my MyMilo?** Only accounts you allow and
devices you connect. The Devices page shows every connected
phone — refresh its token or remove it there, any time.
