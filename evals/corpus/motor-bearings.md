# Electric Motor — Bearing Vibration Monitoring

Rolling-element bearing condition is assessed through vibration velocity
trending per ISO 10816 zone boundaries. For motors in the 15 to 300 kW
range on rigid foundations:
- Zone A/B boundary: 2.8 mm/s RMS — new or recommissioned machines
- Zone B/C boundary: 4.5 mm/s RMS — acceptable for unrestricted operation
- Zone C/D boundary: 7.1 mm/s RMS — plan corrective action; do not run
  to failure beyond this point

Measurement points: drive-end and non-drive-end bearing housings, in
three axes. Trend monthly; a doubling of velocity between readings
escalates the work order regardless of absolute zone.

Grease replenishment follows the bearing manufacturer's calculated
interval, typically 4000 to 8000 hours for standard frames. Over-greasing
causes more bearing failures than under-greasing — apply the calculated
quantity only, and never mix grease thickener types.
