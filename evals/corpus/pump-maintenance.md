# Centrifugal Pump — Mechanical Seal Maintenance

The mechanical seal is the primary wear component on end-suction centrifugal
pumps in continuous process service. The recommended seal replacement
interval is 18000 operating hours under clean fluid conditions.

Seal failure modes to watch during routine inspection:
- Face wear beyond 2 mm indicated by rising leakage at the gland
- O-ring hardening after prolonged exposure above 120 C
- Spring fatigue causing uneven face loading and chatter

Preventive actions: verify seal flush flow at every monthly walkdown,
record leakage rate in the pump log, and trend bearing housing temperature
alongside seal condition. A sudden leakage increase above 10 mL per hour
triggers a planned seal replacement at the next available outage window.

Barrier fluid systems on dual seals require a level check each shift and
a full fluid replacement every 12 months regardless of condition.
