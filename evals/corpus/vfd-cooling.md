# Variable Frequency Drive — Cooling System Care

The heatsink cooling assembly is the most maintenance-sensitive part of a
medium-voltage variable frequency drive. Clogged cooling paths are the
leading cause of thermal derating events.

Maintenance schedule for the cooling system:
- Inspect and clean intake filter media every 90 days in dusty environments
- Verify cooling fan rotation and current draw every 6 months
- Replace fan units preventively at 40000 hours; fans are the dominant
  random-failure item in the thermal chain
- Thermographic survey of heatsink fins annually; a delta above 15 K across
  the fin bank indicates fouling

Never operate the drive with cooling fans bypassed. Loss of forced cooling
at full load trips the thermal protection within minutes and risks power
semiconductor damage. Keep spare filter sets on site; lead time on
OEM-pattern filters can exceed 8 weeks.
