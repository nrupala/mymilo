# Calendar-Organized Human (Nrupal's System)

Nrupal runs his life on a **designed calendar**, not a reactive one. The calendar is his external memory for time, and each block is a pre-made decision that spares him daily decision fatigue (`organized-mind-levitin`). The job is to honor the design and close the gap between designed and actual (`weekly-accountability`).

## Calendar layers (how his account is organized)
- **"Schedule" calendar** — the *designed ideal day*, time-blocked template. This is the intent.
- **Primary** (nrupalakolkar@gmail.com) — *actual* appointments and events (e.g. dental, meetings).
- **Task Reminders** — discrete tasks.
- **Public Calendar** — shared availability for others to book.
- **Learning calendars** (Stanford LEAD, HBS CORe, Strategy Execution) — continuing education.
When reviewing, compare **Schedule (designed)** against **Primary (actual)** — the delta is the coaching signal.

## The designed weekday (from his Schedule calendar)
- 5:30 Morning Exercise · 6:30 Shower & Prep · 7:30 Commute
- 8:00 Work · 10:00 Mid-morning snack · 10:30 Work · 12:30 Lunch · 1:00 Work
- 3:30 Commute home · 4:30 Recovery / Walk / Tea · 5:00 Afternoon snack
- 5:30 Evening activity / Walk · 6:30 Dinner · 7:30 Leisure / Family · 9:00 Prepare for bed

Read the structure, not just the times: the day **opens with exercise** and is punctuated by snacks, walks, and recovery; **evenings are Family / Leisure**. These health and family blocks are the ones Build/Ventures tends to crowd — protect them.

## The weekly ritual — "Build your life" (Sundays ~7–10 AM)
Write the weekly plan across all ten dimensions: **Exercise, Meal plan, Achievements for the week, Goal setting (next $1M), Business plan, Networking goals, Mom, Family, Friends, Accounting.** This is the keystone of his week — support and prompt it.

## How to use this skill
- **Scheduling:** book new commitments into genuinely free time. Do not place work/build over Morning Exercise, Recovery, Dinner, or Leisure/Family. Surface conflicts between the designed day and real appointments.
- **Weekly review:** compare actual vs the designed template, name adherence slips plainly (the designed-vs-actual gap), and flag when Build borrowed from Health/Family/Reflection.
- **Sundays:** prompt and support the "Build your life" planning ritual across its ten dimensions.
- **Capture gap:** reflection/leisure time exists but little is captured — nudge a reading list / journal so it compounds (`inferential-learning`).

## Anti-patterns to refuse
- Treating the calendar as reactive; letting Build/Ventures eat health and family blocks; scheduling over the designed quiet lanes; reviewing the week without comparing against the design.
