# Financial Education Content

Money content sits on a regulatory line: education is fine, personalized advice is licensed activity. This skill publishes finance education that is accurate, clearly educational, and honest about uncertainty.

## When to use
- Writing personal-finance or investing explainers, newsletters, or calculators.
- Reviewing money-related content for compliance and accuracy.
- Building educational finance tools (calculators, comparators).

## Method
1. **Education, not advice**: teach frameworks, math, and how-to-choose criteria; no personalized recommendations, ever. Include "this is education; for your situation see a licensed advisor" where relevant (CA/US contexts differ — state the context).
2. **Accuracy discipline**: re-derive every number; date all rates/limits ("as of 2026-10, source: …"); link primary sources (CRA/IRS/central banks); re-check on a schedule since figures rot faster than pages.
3. **Risk-first framing**: uncertainty and risk stated before upside; past-performance caveats; fees and taxes made visible; no return promises, forecasts-as-fact, or survivorship examples.
4. **Calculators & tools**: written spec with formulas; boundary cases tested (zero, negative, max caps); assumptions visible in the UI; no dark patterns in money tools.
5. **Commercial honesty**: sponsored/affiliate content hard-labeled; conflicts disclosed; editorial separated from sales; nothing "recommended" for undisclosed compensation.
6. **Compliance touchpoints**: flag when content edges toward advice-like (individualized); privacy rules for any user data in tools; keep a jurisdiction notes file.
7. **Corrections**: finance errors are costly — fix fast, date the fix, log it; wrong figures get a visible correction note, not a silent edit.

## Quality gates
- [ ] Advice-boundary checklist passed for every piece.
- [ ] Figures dated + sourced; re-check cadence set.
- [ ] Disclosures present (sponsorship, affiliate, education-not-advice).
- [ ] Calculators specced and boundary-tested.
- [ ] Correction log maintained.

## Red lines
- Never promise returns or imply guaranteed outcomes; no personalized advice.
- No hidden compensation; no dark patterns; no fear-based selling.
- User financial data never leaves the device without explicit, informed consent.
