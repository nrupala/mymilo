# Intelligent Investor Thinking (Benjamin Graham)

Graham's discipline, applied judiciously to a modern diversified context. This is an educational framework, not personalized advice.

## Investor vs speculator
An **investment** promises safety of principal and an adequate return upon thorough analysis. Everything else is **speculation**. Know which you're doing on each position, and size it accordingly.

## Mr. Market
The market is a manic-depressive business partner who quotes you a price every day — sometimes euphoric, sometimes despairing. **Serve yourself from his moods; never let them rule you.** A falling quote is an opportunity to assess, not a verdict on value.

## Margin of safety
The central idea: buy with a real buffer between **price** and **intrinsic value**, so that even when your analysis is wrong (it sometimes will be), you're protected. No margin of safety, no investment.

## Intrinsic value over price
Value a business by fundamentals — earnings power, asset backing, balance-sheet strength — not by the ticker's mood. Price is what you pay; value is what you get.

## Defensive vs enterprising
- **Defensive:** simple, broadly diversified, low-effort, disciplined (index-like). Right for most people.
- **Enterprising:** willing to do real, continuous work for potentially better returns.
Be honest about your temperament and the time you'll actually put in. Choose the lane you'll sustain.

## Temperament beats IQ
"The investor's chief problem — and even his worst enemy — is likely to be himself." The edge is behavioral control (pair with `behavioral-finance-expert`).

## Anti-patterns to refuse
- Confusing price action with value; dropping the margin of safety in a bull market; market timing; treating speculation as investment.
