# Procurement & Vendor Management

Buy deliberately: spec first, source honestly, receive verified, pay matched. Vendors are long relationships — manage them like assets, not transactions.

## When to use
- Buying equipment, services, or software of any material size.
- Selecting or renewing vendors; managing deliveries and invoices.
- Annual vendor reviews; fixing billing chaos.

## Method
1. **Spec before shopping**: functional + compliance + interface requirements written; buy to spec, not to brochure (`engineering-work-packages` for technical buys).
2. **Sourcing**: three-quote discipline for material spend (same scope, quantities, delivery, terms, warranty); single-source decisions justified in writing.
3. **Evaluate total cost, not price**: acquisition + operations + spares + support + switching cost; weighted criteria set BEFORE scoring (`decision-making`).
4. **Quote & contract care**: read exclusions, payment terms, penalties, warranty, price-escalation clauses; binding terms get review (`legal-licensing`; counsel for contracts).
5. **PO discipline**: PO before delivery; changes via change order; no verbal commitments — or paper them same day.
6. **Receipt & reconciliation**: **3-way match** (PO ↔ receipt/inspection ↔ invoice) before payment; discrepancies resolved first; inspect against spec on arrival, not after install.
7. **Vendor records**: terms, contacts, performance log (on-time, quality, responsiveness); annual review; keep two viable sources for critical items where feasible.
8. **Fraud hygiene**: banking-detail changes verified via known contacts (never email links); segregation where possible; gifts/conflict policy respected.

## Quality gates
- [ ] Spec existed before quotes; TCO evaluation documented.
- [ ] Quotes comparable; exclusions reviewed; single-source justified.
- [ ] 3-way match enforced; nothing paid without receipt verification.
- [ ] Vendor performance logged; review cadence set.

## Red lines
- No PO-less purchases above threshold; no payment without delivery confirmation; no silent bank-detail changes — verify through known channels always.
