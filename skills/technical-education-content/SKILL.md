# Technical Education Content

Teaching is compression that must not lie. This skill is for explainers that make technical topics (energy, stats, AI, finance) understandable to non-specialists without becoming wrong.

## When to use
- Writing explainers, FAQs, newsletters, or educational product content.
- Simplifying a technical concept for a decision-maker or the public.
- Editing someone else's explainer for accuracy.

## Method
1. **Audience & outcome**: define what the reader should be able to *do or say* afterwards; state assumed prior knowledge (none assumed by default).
2. **Spine**: question → why it matters → one core idea → how it works → where it breaks → what to watch. One idea per piece; split if two.
3. **Simplification contract**: simplify language, never facts; explicitly flag what was simplified and how; keep units, orders of magnitude, and directions (up/down) correct.
4. **Analogies that confess**: one analogy, stated with its failure mode ("like a postal system, except nobody signs for delivery").
5. **Concrete numbers & cases**: real figures with dates & sources, worked mini-examples, visuals where they carry weight.
6. **Verification loop**: every factual claim labeled & sourced; safety-relevant topics (electrical, medical, money) get expert review and "defers to licensed practice" notes.
7. **Re-render, don't rewrite**: one canonical explainer → FAQ/thread/video-script renderings (audience-contracts thinking); update the source, regenerate.

## Quality gates
- [ ] Accuracy review done (sources archived; claims labeled).
- [ ] Simplifications flagged; analogies carry their limits.
- [ ] Reading level appropriate; jargon defined on first use.
- [ ] Safety-critical content reviewed by a qualified person where applicable.

## Red lines
- No invented numbers or "studies show" without a study; no fear-mongering or hype.
- Never teach beyond competence — get review or don't publish; electrical/power content never encourages unlicensed practice.
- Corrections follow the verification skill: dated, visible, logged.
