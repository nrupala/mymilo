👋 The MyMilo guide

What Milo can do, how to ask, and where to look when something puzzles you. The same guide lives inside the app under Help & guide.

Quick start

  1. Connect your phone. On the Devices page, create a token, then paste it into the app's Connect screen. (Soon: sign-in pairing — no pasting.)
  2. Ask anything. Type in the message box, or tap 🎤 and talk. Short or long — Milo keeps the thread.
  3. Summon Milo anywhere. Once you've made Milo your assistant (app drawer → Assistant), long-press Home or swipe up from a bottom corner and just speak.
  4. Run a skill on purpose. Open the Skills catalogue below — pick one, and tell it what to work on.
  5. Check the sources. Under each answer, Milo names what it used — a skill, your memory, the web, live market data. Nothing used, nothing claimed.

Questions, answered

Where do Milo's answers come from?

From Milo's own server (Aetheris) — small, fast local models for everyday questions, stronger routes when a task needs them, plus live web search and market data when freshness matters. Every answer lists its sources.

What works without internet?

Calculations and unit conversions (instant, on the phone), all your saved chats, and the skills list. Questions that need the server are kept and answered when you're back — the app tells you instead of failing silently.

What does Milo remember about me?

Facts you share and the shape of past conversations, kept on your server — never sold, never used for ads (there are no ads). You can see and delete remembered facts from your profile.

What exactly is a skill?

A playbook: a body of know-how — a method, a checklist, a body of expertise — that Milo applies to your request. There are dozens, shelved by topic in the catalogue above. In the app, open Skills, tap one, and tell it what to work on.

How do updates arrive?

The app checks quietly and tells you in the drawer under Updates. Download, then Install — Android asks for your tap on the installer; that's the platform's rule for apps outside the Play Store, not a Milo limitation.

Who else can use my MyMilo?

Only accounts you allow and devices you connect. The Devices page shows every connected phone — give one a fresh token or remove it there, any time.

Is Milo the same as the AI in my other apps?

No — Milo runs on your own server, with your data under your control, and skills built around your work. Same family of technology, very different ownership.
